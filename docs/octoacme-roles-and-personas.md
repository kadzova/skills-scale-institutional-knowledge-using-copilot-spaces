# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality standards, plan testing strategies, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria and quality gates before release. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure testability and risk mitigation.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test plans and test cases for features
- Define quality acceptance criteria and testing strategy
- Execute or coordinate manual and automated testing
- Identify defects, prioritize, and track resolution
- Validate compliance and security requirements
- Provide quality metrics and risk reports to the PM

### Goals
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases
- Ensure customer satisfaction and reliability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly QA status in delivery syncs
- Test case reviews during planning
- Bug reports and risk escalations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Reviews test plans and collaborates on test case design; provides feedback on defects and acceptance criteria validation
- **Product Managers**: Validates feature acceptance and reports quality metrics to inform go/no-go decisions
- **Project Managers**: Escalates blockers and quality risks that impact timeline

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction, evaluate design trade-offs, and guide the development team on architecture and engineering practices. They serve as mentors and decision-makers for technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns
- Review and approve significant technical decisions
- Evaluate technology choices and dependencies
- Mentor developers and conduct design reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure code quality, maintainability, and scalability

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Enable team growth and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review guidance and mentoring
- Risk identification and technical trade-off analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, mentors on best practices, and reviews technical decisions
- **Product Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and trade-offs during planning
- **Project Managers**: Identifies and escalates technical risks and dependencies

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key stakeholders provide business context, approve major decisions, and govern resource allocation. They serve as the voice of organizational priorities and strategic alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve project charter, major scope changes, and milestone decisions
- Allocate and authorize resources
- Resolve escalated conflicts and trade-offs
- Provide feedback and strategic guidance
- Communicate project status to broader organization

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value
- Maintain strategic alignment across initiatives
- Mitigate organizational risks

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates
- Project approval and decision gates
- Escalation resolution (Level 3 in escalation ladder)

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Aligns on business objectives and approves prioritization decisions
- **Project Managers**: Approves resource allocation, resolves escalations, and receives executive status updates
- **Developers/Technical Leads**: Provides strategic context for technical decisions and trade-offs

---

## Product Lead / Head of Product

### Role Summary
Product Leads oversee the product vision, manage cross-project prioritization, and align business strategy with product execution. They serve as the senior accountability layer for product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Set and communicate product vision and strategy
- Prioritize across multiple projects and initiatives
- Align product roadmap with business goals
- Mentor Product Managers and guide prioritization decisions
- Approve significant scope, timeline, or resource changes
- Own product-level metrics and outcome tracking

### Goals
- Maximize product-market fit and competitive advantage
- Ensure consistent, strategic product evolution
- Develop product management capability within the team

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Roadmap reviews and prioritization sessions
- Escalation resolution (Level 2 in escalation ladder)

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Mentors on prioritization and strategy; approves major backlog and roadmap decisions
- **Project Managers**: Aligns projects with strategic priorities; resolves cross-project conflicts and dependencies
- **Stakeholders**: Represents product voice to executive leadership on strategic alignment

---

## Communications / Documentation Owner

### Role Summary
Communications Owners ensure that project information, decisions, and updates are documented, discoverable, and communicated to relevant audiences. They maintain the single source of truth for project artifacts and status.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain project documentation (charter, plans, retrospectives)
- Draft and distribute stakeholder communication updates
- Ensure release notes and change documentation are current
- Organize and archive decision logs and artifacts
- Coordinate with teams to gather status information
- Ensure documentation aligns with process standards

### Goals
- Reduce information silos and improve knowledge discovery
- Enable consistent, timely stakeholder communication
- Build an institutional knowledge base for continuous improvement

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status compilations and stakeholder reports
- Documentation templates and maintenance
- Communication review with PM and PdM

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Partners on gathering and distributing project status; maintains risk and decision logs
- **Product Managers**: Ensures feature specs and acceptance criteria are documented and discoverable
- **Developers**: Captures technical decisions and design documentation for knowledge base
- **All Roles**: Ensures consistent, timely communication across stakeholders
